## Platform

## Users

Filipino homeowners exploring a first home or cosmetic renovation. The user confirmed that welcoming, clear, easy-to-use homeowner planning should lead this redesign.

## Product Purpose

Help homeowners understand a preliminary construction budget, compare floor area and finish choices, and keep estimates for later conversations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Repository-backed capabilities: guided estimator, budget-to-build planner, scenario optimizer, five material calculators, authenticated private projects, and generated PDF reports. Preserve these working flows and their calculations.

Rates are educational demonstration assumptions, not current market prices. Estimates are not professional quotations, measured bills of quantities, permits, or signed engineering documents. Keep these limitations visible. Accounts are required for private project storage and PDF reports; guest estimates stay in the current browser tab. Paid services and professional consultations remain upcoming.
